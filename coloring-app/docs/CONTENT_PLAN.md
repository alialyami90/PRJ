# Content Plan: 8 regions × 8 scenes

Every scene is a full *scene* (place + people/animals + activity), never an isolated object. Target age 4–9. Keep lines bold and regions large enough for small fingers; 20–60 fill regions per scene.

Each scene has a **character**: a person in the region's customary clothing, or an animal from that region, drawn as the focus of the picture. Each scene is tagged **traditional** or **modern** so every region shows both its heritage and today's life (aim for roughly half and half per region; see the tally at the bottom).

Clothing rule: in a *traditional* scene the clothing is the region's own dress (not a generic thobe everywhere; Asir, Hijaz, Jazan and Al-Ahsa each have distinct dress). In a *modern* scene the clothing is everyday modern wear, and any thobe/abaya is a plain contemporary cut.

Status per scene lives in `Content/Regions/<region>/region.json` (`todo` → `sketched` → `vectorized` → `approved`).
