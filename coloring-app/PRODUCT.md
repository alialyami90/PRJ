# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Users
Children aged 4 to 9 in Saudi Arabia and the wider Gulf (age band 6 to 8 on the App Store), coloring on an iPad (primary) or iPhone, often on the sofa or at a table, sometimes with a parent nearby. Many cannot read yet or read Arabic before English. Parents are a second audience: they pass the parental gate, judge whether the app is safe and culturally right, and choose to keep it installed.

## Product Purpose
"Saudi Colors" is a coloring book of the Kingdom: 9 regions, 8 full scenes each (place, people or animals, activity). A child picks a region, picks a page, and colors it in one of two modes: Fill (tap a shape) and Paint (brush). Paint by number was tried and dropped in October 2026. Success is a child finishing pages, coming back to the next region, and a parent seeing their own culture shown with care.

## Positioning
The only coloring book whose pages are the real places, dress, crafts and celebrations of each Saudi region, drawn as complete scenes. Each region is a different world (Najd, Hijaz, Al-Ahsa, Asir, AlUla, Tabuk, Jazan, Hail, Najran), and the app teaches that difference by letting the child color it.

## Operating Context
Used in short sessions, landscape iPad by default, by small fingers. Fill is taps, Paint is drags; pinch zooms. Work saves on leaving. Ads appear only on the gallery banner and an optional rewarded "more colours" after a finished page.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Native SwiftUI, iOS/iPadOS 17+, project generated by XcodeGen from `project.yml`.
- The Engine (region map, coloring state, renderer) stays free of SwiftUI and is unit tested.
- Kids Category: no behavioural ads, no IDFA, parental gate before anything that leaves the app or plays an ad on request.
- Arabic is first class: strings localizable, RTL-safe layout (leading/trailing only).
- Touch targets at least 60pt in child-facing UI; no text-only buttons for children (icon plus label).
- No em-dashes in user-facing copy; short, friendly words for ages 4 to 9.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: "Saudi Colors" (Arabic display name not yet decided).
- Scene art is a bold black line, flat-colour cartoon style from an illustrator; the UI must frame it, never compete with it.
- Brief from the owner (October 2026): the UI should come from Saudi culture and Saudi design language. The owner chose one unified Saudi look for every region, not a different look per region; the variety lives in the pages.

- No testimonials, ratings, press, or usage numbers exist. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. The child's picture is the hero; every screen exists to get them to it or to celebrate it.
2. Moving between regions should feel like travel through one Kingdom: one shared look, with each region's own pictures and names carrying its character.
3. Respect over decoration: cultural forms are used as they are, never as costume or caricature.
4. A four-year-old who cannot read must be able to use every child-facing screen.
5. Grown-up things (gate, ads, unlocking) stay quiet and clearly separate from play.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Pre-readers: icons and colour carry meaning alongside short labels. Large targets, VoiceOver labels on every control, Dynamic Type for text that adults read, Reduce Motion honoured. Arabic RTL mirroring.
